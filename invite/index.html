How to join

  1. Get pastebook on your iPhone. (Coming soon to the App Store.)
  2. Open it and choose your name. If the invitation was sent to your email, sign in with that email in You → Account & sync.
  3. Go to You → Invitations and choose Join scrapbook.
Invitations last seven days. Only accept invitations from people you know. If yours has expired, ask them to send a new one.

Need help? Write to support@pastebook.app.
