How can we help?

Write to support@pastebook.app. A real person reads every message. In the app, You → Contact support starts an email for you.

Common questions

Where is the invitation someone sent me?

Open You → Invitations. If it was sent to your email, first add or sign in with that email in You → Account & sync. Invitations last seven days.

How do I make a scrapbook with other people?

Open the scrapbook, tap ⋯ → Scrapbook settings → Invite people, and find them by username or email.

How do I open my scrapbooks on another iPhone?

Add an email in You → Account & sync, then sign in with it on the other iPhone. Scrapbooks you made together come with you; ones kept only on the first iPhone stay there.

Someone is being unkind. What can I do?

Tap their piece or note, open ⋯, and choose Report or Block. Blocking is private. You can also leave the scrapbook. We review reports within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Open You → Delete account. The app shows exactly what will be removed before you confirm. See the Privacy policy for details.
